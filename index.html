About Taynz.

A gentle cycle and wellbeing companion. Not a medical device and not a substitute for professional care.

Seek medical advice for symptoms that concern you. Get urgent care for very heavy bleeding, fainting, severe or sudden pain, or possible pregnancy with pain or bleeding. Bleeding that soaks through a pad or tampon every hour for 2 or more hours, especially with dizziness or shortness of breath, needs urgent medical assessment.
